About

Parlo is private, offline dictation for Windows. Speech models: Underdog Bark 3.0 and 3.0 Plus by Conway Research (CC-BY-4.0, derived from NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 and moondream/parakeet-ultra) and OpenAI Whisper via faster-whisper (MIT). Read aloud uses Windows voices. An independent app inspired by Spokenly and Wispr Flow; not affiliated with either.
